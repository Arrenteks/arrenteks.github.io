Fallen Light

Fallen Light is the story about a world trapped in eternal darkness and ice. Every year angels, called the Lightbringers, descend down to the planet to bring warmth and life. This year is Azazel's turn, but something goes horribly wrong, an apocalyptic event known as the ‚Eclipse‘ blows out his guiding beacons and he plummets to the ground. He awakes alone and disoriented. He now needs to strike out on his own and fight the encroaching darkness.

Retrospective and Learnings

Fallen Light was one of my first projects I undertook by myself during my studies. During that time I was, having recently finished Hollow Knight, fascinated with Metroidvanias. Luckily during that time I was participating in a module called 'Mediengestaltung 3' - Media Creation 3, where we were to create our own IP and world for a game. So I did just that. I am still quite happy with how Fallen Light turned out. It is a good idea, that still gives a lot of room for both Gameplay and Narrative Freedom. I still perticularly like the potential for openess and feeling of achievement a world of ice could bring to the player. I could see myself revisiting this project during another time and working out some of the more rough edges.

What did this project teach me?

  • Basics Structures for a coherent world with room for both Gameplay and Narrative
  • Effective Usage of Personas in Game Design
  • Identification and Analysis of Competition
  • The Value of contrasts in Storytelling and Visual Design
