[Ex]Change

Take control of a little cleaning robot in a futuristic,post-apocalyptic world which used to be inhabited by humans. When your peaceful live of cleaning the old house suddenly is interrupted by the key to total adaptability it is your opportunity and burden to save all of robot kind from the AI which already got rid of all humans.


Exchange was an international project between the Hochschule Harz Wernigerode, the Tokyo University of Technology and the Universidad Nacional Autónoma de México. It had a duration of 1 year and focused on game development in a multi-cultural and international team.

  • Genre: Adventure
  • Engine: Unity Game Engine
  • Role(s): Gameplay Programmer
  • Team Size: 8
  • Development Time: 10 months

My Contributions

Programming

  • Implementation of enemy behaviours based on State Machines

  • Implementation of some gameplay items, like a grappling hook

Retrospective and Learnings

The Exchange Project was my first international project at the Hochschule Harz in collaboration with the Tokyo University of Technology and the Universidad Nacional Autónoma de México. It took place over the course of two semesters in what is ominously called the "Jahresprojekt" - Year Long Project. Looking back the thing I learned most was how to communicate in a team. The Exchange Project suffered a lot under communication issues within the german team, let alone with the international team members. I distinctly remember one Mexican student expressed being intimidated by the speed the german students would advance in the project and thus not involving himself in the project. Now I can see, how I could pick him up and try to encourage him, but back then I was way more timid and did not know how to approach the situation. Exchange is one of my university projects I recall as being the most taxing and straining during my Bachelor's Degree. While the Game turned out fine in the end, there was a lot of untapped potential within. Since communication was sparse until the end, I can see how a lot of potential simply vanished over of non-communication.

If I look at it with my current perspective, I would try and really get everyone on one page before even beginning development. International understanding is imparative, when working in such a team and I think we performed suboptimally in that regard. Exchange is definitely one of the projects, that shaped the way I approach collaboration the most, hence why its place in this portfolio.

Learnings from this project

  • Research other cultures, if you are to work with them
  • Be approachable by anyone
  • There always needs to be a glue, holding the people of the project together
  • Talk to people, even if it is just smalltalk
  • Do not disregard differing opinions
